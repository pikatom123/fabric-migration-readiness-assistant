TWO-MINUTE PROTOTYPE WALKTHROUGH
1. Open index.html in Edge or Chrome and select Reset.
2. Explain the three intake paths: manual form, Excel saved as CSV, or PDF through Copilot/CLI.
3. Select Upload PDF or CSV and choose Contoso_Estate_Inventory.csv.
4. Select Continue and point out that imported rows are Needs Discovery, not guessed as ready.
5. Open Add Workload to show manual conversational discovery fields.
6. Select Load sample to show a completed assessment with confirmed facts.
7. Open Assessment and expand every workload to show blockers, optimisation actions, and questions.
8. Open Summary to show executive position, target patterns, next steps, and SKU Estimator inputs.
9. Close on the roadmap for Snowflake, deeper Databricks telemetry, AWS/GCP, Oracle, and Teradata.

IMPORTANT DEMO LANGUAGE
- Say "Excel inventory saved as CSV"; the browser does not natively parse .xlsx.
- Say "directional SKU Estimator inputs," not "capacity quote."
- PDF extraction runs through the CLI or Copilot workflow, not inside the browser-only page.
